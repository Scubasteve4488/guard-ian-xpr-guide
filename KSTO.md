# KSTO: what the community's own site says

Summarised in our own words from ksto.world. **All pages read 2026-10-15.** The site's only date is a "2026" copyright line. Check the linked page before quoting anything as current.

## What KSTO is
- KSTO is a community-driven movement built around the **$KSTO** token. It values authenticity and transparency over hype ([About](https://ksto.world/about.html), [FAQ](https://ksto.world/faq_v1.html)).
- Its mascot is **Snippy the Guardian** ([About](https://ksto.world/about.html)).
- The site is credited to **Mimi Mesh, LLC** ([mimimesh.com](https://mimimesh.com)).
- The home page groups $KSTO into four parts ([home](https://ksto.world/index.html)):
  - **Utility:** staking, snipping streaks, planned tools.
  - **Community:** Telegram, X and Snipverse.
  - **Governance:** DAO proposals in the open.
  - **Culture:** Snippy and memes.

## The ecosystem, as the site lists it ([Ecosystem](https://ksto.world/ecosystem.html))
- **Connect:** Telegram, X Spaces, community calls, AMAs, "Morning Vibes".
- **Earn:** Snippy Streaks, token rains, TipBot rewards, reward pools, events and challenges.
- **Grow:** Blastpad, staking, liquidity pools, farming.
- **Own:** Genesis NFTs, Snippy NFTs, NFT packs, an NFT Claim Hub.
- **Build:** education, community growth, governance.

## Governance ([Governance](https://ksto.world/governance.html))
- The site describes KSTO as community-governed, with transparent, on-chain proposals and votes.
- It links a White Paper and a Constitution.
- The page does **not** name the voting platform or say who is eligible to propose or vote. Say "not stated on the site" rather than guessing.

## Vetted tokens ([Vetted Tokens](https://ksto.world/vetted_tokens.html))
- The site lists 11 hand-picked community tokens that fit KSTO's values of transparency, community strength and real utility:
  - SNIPPY, MARSH, HEAL, PBR, LBUG, SNIP, LADY, PAPA, BENDER, STRAZ, GUARD.
- The page does not explain how vetting is done.
- GUARD is one of the eleven, equal to the rest. KSTO is the community; Snippy is its main mascot.
